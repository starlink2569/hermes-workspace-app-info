Hermes Workspace

Google Workspace automation operated by its owner, for the owner’s requested tasks.

Hermes Workspace is a personal, self-hosted application. It is not a public signup service, an official Google product, or a service hosted by Nous Research.

This is an information site, not a login page. It does not collect Google credentials or Google account data. Do not enter or post private information here or in public support issues.

What the application does

  • Runs owner-requested tasks in Google Drive, Gmail, Calendar, Contacts, Docs, and Sheets, only as allowed by the permissions the owner grants.
  • Stores age-encrypted backups in Google Drive, retaining three restore points. This backup policy is not a three-day retention rule for all logs or data.
  • Uses owner-controlled local resources. Depending on the requested task and configuration, some Google data may also be sent to an AI provider selected by the owner for processing.

Permissions and control

Google authorization is handled separately through Google’s consent flow. Access depends on the granted scopes; granting a scope does not mean every task needs all data accessible through it. The application uses the data needed for the owner’s requested task.

Read the Privacy policy for data handling, retention, revocation, and deletion, and the Terms of use for the operator’s responsibilities.

Support and questions

The owner can use the support email shown on the Google consent screen or the project issue tracker. Public issues are not a private support channel: do not include account data, message contents, credentials, tokens, or backup contents.
