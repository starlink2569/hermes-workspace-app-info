Data handling

Privacy policy

This policy describes the personal, owner-operated Hermes Workspace deployment, not a public hosted service.

Last reviewed: November 13, 2026

This public information site

These static pages contain no OAuth login form and do not collect Google credentials or Google account data. They contain no analytics, tracking scripts, cookies, or forms. The site’s hosting provider may process ordinary request information under its own terms; these pages do not control hosting-provider logs.

Do not post private information, credentials, tokens, or Google account content in public issues.

Google access and task use

The application accesses Google data only within the scopes the owner grants and uses the minimum data needed for the requested task. Depending on those permissions and the task, this can include Drive files and metadata; Gmail messages and metadata; Calendar events; Contacts; and Docs or Sheets content.

Tasks can read, create, update, send, or delete information where the granted scopes permit. The owner should review permissions and requested actions before execution. This deployment does not sell Google data or use it for advertising.

Where processing happens

The application uses local resources controlled by the owner and Google services needed for the task. Google data is not guaranteed to remain entirely local: when the owner configures an AI provider for a requested task, relevant data may be sent to that provider for processing.

This deployment uses configured AI processing to carry out requested tasks, not as a pipeline for training models. An external provider’s handling and retention depend on its terms and the owner’s account settings. The owner should review those terms and settings before allowing sensitive data to be processed.

Credentials and local security

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored locally with restricted access under the owner’s control, not in these public pages. The owner is responsible for protecting the host, restricting local access, and managing credential rotation or replacement when needed. Authorization may expire or be revoked; continued access is not guaranteed.

Logs, backups, and retention

Local files, task outputs, and logs are retained according to the owner’s configuration and cleanup controls. Logs may contain task-related information; the owner should limit logging and access as appropriate. This policy does not promise a fixed retention period for every data type.

Drive backups are encrypted with age and retain three restore points. Three restore points do not mean three days, and this policy does not imply that all logs or other copies are deleted after three days. The owner controls the backup decryption material and backup deletion.

Revocation and deletion

The owner can revoke Google access in Google account connections. Revocation stops automations that depend on that access. It does not itself delete data already stored locally, in Drive, or with a configured provider.

To remove retained data, the owner must use local deletion controls for files, logs, task outputs, and credentials, and Google Drive controls for backups and other stored files, including trash where applicable. Provider-held data is subject to that provider’s deletion controls, retention terms, and account settings. Stopping scheduled tasks may also be necessary to prevent new copies.

Questions and policy updates

The owner can use the support email displayed on the Google consent screen or the project issue tracker. Do not share private data in public issues. Review this policy again when application permissions or processing settings change.

See also the Terms of use and application overview.
